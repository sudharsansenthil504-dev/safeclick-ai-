About SafeClick AI

SafeClick AI is a free, mobile-friendly website designed to help people in India pause and review common warning signs before responding to suspicious messages, job offers or website links.

What you can check

  • Scam messages: common clues such as OTP or password requests, urgent payment demands, threats and unexpected rewards.
  • Job offers: possible recruitment fees, requests for money and unrealistic promises.
  • Website links: visible URL warning signs. The checker examines the text of a URL and does not open the destination website.
  • Mobile number and SIM guidance: links to official services for checking connections issued in your own name and reporting suspicious communications.
  • Emergency help: guidance to report financial cyber fraud in India through 1930 and the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal.

Privacy-first approach

The message, job-offer text and URL entered into the checkers are analysed in your browser and are not sent to a SafeClick AI server. The site does not require an account or ask you to submit your phone number. Read the Privacy Policy for more information.

Languages

The interface supports English, Tamil, Hindi, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Bengali, Marathi, Gujarati, Punjabi and Urdu. Some detection clues are language-specific, so detection coverage is not complete in every language.

Important limitations

SafeClick AI uses basic warning-sign rules. It is not a live threat-intelligence service, caller identification service, official fraud verification service or guarantee. It can miss scams and can flag harmless content. A low-risk result does not prove that something is genuine. Always verify independently through an organisation's official website or contact channel.

Never share OTPs, UPI PINs, passwords, bank details or remote-access permissions with an unknown person. If you lose money to online financial fraud in India, call 1930 immediately and report it at cybercrime.gov.in.
