Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

SafeClick AI is a free, browser-based early-warning tool that helps people review possible warning signs in messages, job offers and website links. This policy explains what happens when you use this website.

1. Information you enter into the checkers

The message text, job-offer text and URL that you enter into the checkers are analysed locally in your browser. SafeClick AI does not send this checker input to a SafeClick AI server. The site does not ask you to create an account or submit your phone number.

2. Language preference

If you change the website language, your selected language may be saved in your browser's local storage so the site can remember that preference on your next visit. This preference stays in your browser unless you clear the site's stored data.

3. Cookies, analytics and advertising

SafeClick AI does not currently use its own advertising cookies or analytics scripts, and the website does not currently display advertisements. If analytics or advertising services are added in the future, this policy will be updated to explain the relevant data use and choices before or when those services are introduced.

4. Website hosting and technical information

This website is hosted using GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process technical information, such as IP address, browser details or request logs, to deliver, protect and maintain its hosting service. Such processing is governed by the provider's own policies. Read the GitHub Privacy Statement.

5. Links to other websites

SafeClick AI may link to external services, including official Government of India cybercrime and telecom services. When you open an external website, that website's own privacy policy and terms apply. SafeClick AI does not control those websites.

6. Children's privacy

SafeClick AI is a general-purpose information and safety tool. It is not designed to collect personal information from children. Do not enter sensitive personal information into any checker.

7. Security and limitations

Checker results are generated using basic warning-sign rules in your browser. They are not a guarantee, identity check or official fraud verification. A low-risk result does not prove that a message, job offer or website is genuine. Avoid entering OTPs, passwords, UPI PINs, bank details or other sensitive information.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if SafeClick AI's features, hosting or data practices change. The latest revision date will be shown at the top of this page.

9. Contact

SafeClick AI does not currently publish a dedicated support email address. Please do not send sensitive personal or financial information through informal channels.

If you have lost money to online financial fraud in India, contact the national cyber-fraud helpline at 1930 immediately and report it at cybercrime.gov.in.
